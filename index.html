About me!

Hey there, I'm Charlene Brown. I'm a Brooklyn-rooted tech strategist, creative problem-solver, and founder of Bklyn Custom Designs®, with multiple years of experience blending tech, brand strategy, and digital innovation. My love for coding started long before today's AI boom, and front-end development has been part of my journey for years.

Now, I'm expanding my developer skills into AI, exploring new ways to build intelligent, accessible, and user-focused digital solutions. I'm a lifelong learner who believes that strategy comes before tools, creativity belongs in tech, and there's always another level to unlock. For me, coding isn't a career pivot. It's part of who I am, and I'm just getting started on what comes next!
